My professional experience sits where software, manufacturing, and people meet. Working with web-based manufacturing execution systems has shown me how much a clear interface and a dependable workflow matter.

Through software implementation, customer support, and project coordination, I've learned to listen to users, understand their needs, and help turn complex processes into practical solutions.

Now, I'm building on that experience through programming and web design. My projects range from Python desktop applications to community websites and registration tools. I enjoy the combination of creative design and logical problem solving that makes an application both useful and easy to navigate.

My goal is to move into user interface and web development, bringing my understanding of real-world software workflows to the applications I create.
