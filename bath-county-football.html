The idea

Project overview

This project page provides a place to showcase the Bath County Football website. Its screenshot gallery will highlight the actual homepage, an additional page, and the experience on a smaller screen.

The goal

Present football information in a clear, visually coordinated website that is easy for visitors to explore.

What it includes

Key features

  • Homepage design and the first impression it creates.
  • Organization of football-related content across the website.
  • Consistent visual styling and navigation.
